Harvard's graduate application has an "Additional Materials" section. I figured I could talk about my experience orthogonal to academia and research but still very meaningful to me and where I spent a lot of time and life force on over the past 2 years.

-----

I’d like to talk briefly about an AI research group I founded called AttentionX ([website link](https://attentionx.notion.site/AttentionX-f13583b9cc6e452dbc8a1a9fec2bac00) in portfolio, which is slightly outdated).

For context, I went back to Korea to do mandatory alternative military service for 2 years starting in 2024 and I’ve lived in the US since I was 2 years old so I didn’t know anyone when I went back.

But to make the best out of this situation and to continue with my AI research journey after leaving Berkeley, I started an AI research group called AttentionX based in Seoul, working on research projects with other undergraduate and graduate students from Seoul National University (SNU) and KAIST. 

After I gathered 20 students as the initial cohort of very strong computer science and math majors from SNU and KAIST through posting on student internet forums, I reached out to other companies for sponsoring compute for our research projects, invited guest speakers, and divided the group into teams working on different agendas resulting in 4 teams: LLM reasoning, Multimodal LLM, 3D, and diffusion teams.

In 2 years we were able to get 8 publications accepted at NeurIPS, ECCV, ICML, CVPR, and EMNLP working with 4 SNU professors and 2 KAIST professors where I was part of 5 publications. 

We also hosted top AI researchers from industry and academia to our group, such as Naver AI research director, co-hosted AI hackathons with OpenAI, DeepMind, and Anthropic (which also gave us API credits), and also hosted former Harvard professor Julia C. Lee as guest speaker on AI for climate and scientific discovery. 

We’ve also fostered a very strong startup founder pool as well, as 4 teams from our group got funding from YC (YC never funded founders who were attending school in Korea before, until 4 teams came out of our group over the past two years) and one startup called AIM intelligence raised $8M Series A. 

—-

I believe research takes more than quantitative skill to make progress. How you set the culture and environment, even how you design the office and organize events, matters a lot in making a conducive environment to share ideas and pursue projects.

Through this experience, I was in a unique position to build an organization from the ground up and be part of every pipeline from recruiting, getting funding/sponsors and then designing the organization and events, pushing the projects forward to fruition and learned a lot as a leader. 

I have aspirations and ambitions to push the field forward and contribute to the community more broadly beyond myself and my research.
