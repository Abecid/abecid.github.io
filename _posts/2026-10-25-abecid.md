There are very few social environments that this policy is encouraged Nueva school in Palo Alto or if your parents are someone like Paul Graham, Palmer Luckey or Elon Musk. While I claim to be beyond the product of my environment, I might actually still be the product of my environment as well, since my policies were induced and developed from constant friction and learning points in my upbringing, growing up in different regions across Iowa, Vermont, and California. 

So actually we might not be able to escape our own environment after all. But we can still try our best. I think there are two axises to learning and developing from environment feedback.

First, certain environments definitely induce certain capabilites and reasoning. People who had a major tragedy, like a parent passing, becomes a make or break moment. You either become stronger, more motivated and come out on top or you fall to the bottom. So which environments induce certain reasoning and policies intrigue me and in terms of embodied reasoning led me to think more deeply about [human intuition that AI can't mimic, and a new axis of training for embodied reasoning](Intuition). Certain environments definitely have benefits but people who have grown up in that environment don't realize those benefits deeply as humans only learn from pain and failure, there is no incentive or feedback mechanism to update your prior if the policies and traits inherited are optimal for certain objectives -- role of height in charisma/leadership, race, gender.

I'd argue why this isn't as discussed as much is because most people at the frontier are from these backgrounds. (High school student)[Daniel Larsen] making breakthrough in (math area)[] has IMO gold medalist father and fields medalist uncle. If you want to be top-3 in the country, one spot was taken from this group if you're not part of this group, tough luck. It is not impossible to reach this level of success and impact being outside of the dominant cluster, but the odds are very stacked. This is obviously representative of the cluster based accomplishments and breakthroughs that occur widely across fields and industries. 

So most prominent people have an incentive to stay close to their environment and culture as their parents and environment was conducive to their success from Daniel Larsen, Edward Witten, Po-Shen Loh, many people in stem research, hollywood, and wall street. 

Well I don't come from this background, as many others do. So the best policy is to reset. Disown your environment and parents. If you want to excel in math, think of Po-Shen Loh as your father and listen to him more closely than your father. If you want to do startups, think of Paul Graham as your father. Listen to him and follow his advice more closely than your father. These are things I've done, albeit later than I should have starting junior year in high school. 

The next best policy for people who don't come from this cluster is to focus on latest technology. Standardized exams, competitions, college admissions, undergraduate fellowships are so heavily infiltrated these acoomplishments are purchasable and a small clsuter dominate. Even mathematics, which should appear as though is most rigorous, fair and independent of politics and external influences, the Morgan prize winners are dominated by Daniel Larsen, people from Phillips Exceter. This means people outside these clusters are very heavily penalized to accomplishing these. But the one thing these clusters can't dominate is latest technology. They have much less advantage to be the ones making progress in writing viral LLM reasoning papers or unicorn ai startups. Research publishing and papers in it of itself is highly concentrated within certain groups (diffusion reward modeling, step distillation in Albergo, Yilun, Vahdat's groups; systems in Song Han, Ion Stoica's groups). You are still at an disadtantage in publishing being outside of these groups. But these people also has another constraint. They need to build up their research and expertise. If they are not utilizing and building on their prior works, they have no advantage.
